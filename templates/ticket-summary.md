# Ticket Summary

Use this template when presenting a backlog item for confirmation or planning
context.

Read the work item content internally to synthesize the sections below. Do not
dump the raw content unless the developer asks for it.

## Format (Markdown)

```markdown
## <Work item title>

### Objective

<summary of what must be achieved>

### Business Context

<summary of the why and functional context>

### Acceptance Criteria

<summary of verifiable conditions, or "Not specified in the work item">
```

## Rules

* Use the work item title as the title.
* Synthesize only from the work item content.
* Do not display raw provider content or metadata by default.
* State when required information is missing.
