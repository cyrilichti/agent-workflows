title: /plan
description: Turn one delivery unit into an approved implementation plan.
---

`/plan` turns one task into an approved implementation plan. It first checks
that the task is one coherent delivery unit, routes the work to the right
specialist, and resolves every material question before asking for approval.
When planning an official item, the plan carries its exact linked reference;
standalone plans omit that reference.

## What `/plan` does

<table class="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Assess</th>
      <td>Continue for one delivery unit or return <code>needs-refinement</code> without creating a plan.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Plan</th>
      <td>Route to one specialist, inspect the relevant context, and write the plan with the exact official item link when supplied.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Resolve</th>
      <td>Ask and incorporate every answer that could materially change the plan.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Approve</th>
      <td>Present the complete plan and revise it until the human approves autonomous delivery.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
