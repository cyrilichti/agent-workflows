---
title: /write
description: Create or improve one work item with the right writing skill for the current need.
---

`/write` drafts one new or existing item, checks its outcome, observable behavior,
success criteria, constraints, decisions, and open questions, then asks only what
blocks later treatment at the same level of abstraction. After confirmation, it saves
the item and applies `agent-shaped`; a missing ClickUp tag is reported without failing
the save. The label records use of `/write`, not readiness, feasibility, or size.

## How this workflow is structured

<table class="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Mode</th>
      <td>Choose create or reformulate.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Context</th>
      <td>Describe a new need and resolve its destination, or select an existing item and describe the intended reformulation.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Draft</th>
      <td>Present an improved proposal early and identify the most fundamental authoring need.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Guidance</th>
      <td>Use at most one best-fit writing skill and reassess after meaningful input.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>5</td>
      <th scope="row">Review</th>
      <td>Resolve or preserve blockers, review fragile decisions and optional suggestions, then confirm the item.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>6</td>
      <th scope="row">Save</th>
      <td>Save the confirmed content, apply `agent-shaped`, and report the result.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
