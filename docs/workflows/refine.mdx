---
title: /refine
description: Split one oversized work item into confirmed child items without changing the parent.
---

`/refine` decomposes one oversized official item into autonomous child items.
It completes the same item context in both entry modes, including comments,
before assessing or drafting. The parent stays unchanged, and no child is
created before the human confirms the complete decomposition.

## How `/refine` decomposes an item

## What `/refine` does

<table class="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Assess</th>
      <td>Reuse supplied context, read only missing supported information, and stop without changes when the item is one indivisible delivery unit.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Draft</th>
      <td>Use one specialist to draft at least two useful children that cover the parent scope exactly once, preferring vertical feature slices and allowing justified progressive migrations.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Validate</th>
      <td>Return every deficiency to the same specialist until the complete decomposition is valid.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Confirm</th>
      <td>Preview every child and relation, then confirm everything, adjust the draft, or cancel without changes.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>5</td>
      <th scope="row">Create</th>
      <td>Require creation prerequisites first, then create confirmed children in order, copy <code>agent-shaped</code> when required, persist valid blocking relations, continue feasible operations after individual failures, and report successful and failed results separately.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
