title: /refine

`/refine` decomposes one oversized official item into autonomous child items.
The parent stays unchanged, and no child is created before the human confirms
the complete decomposition.

## How `/refine` decomposes an item

## What `/refine` does

<table class="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Assess</th>
      <td>In standalone mode, stop without changes when the item is already one coherent delivery unit.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Draft</th>
      <td>Use one specialist to draft at least two autonomous children that cover the parent scope exactly once.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Validate</th>
      <td>Return every deficiency to the same specialist until the complete decomposition is valid.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Confirm</th>
      <td>Preview every child and relation, then confirm everything, adjust the draft, or cancel without changes.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>5</td>
      <th scope="row">Create</th>
      <td>Create confirmed children in order, copy <code>agent-shaped</code> when required, persist valid blocking relations, and report every result.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
