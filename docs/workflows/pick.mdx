---
title: /pick
description: Pick your next work item, approve its plan, and continue with /work.
---

`/pick` turns a ready item into active work. It lets you choose an item, reviews
its context, and sends it to `/plan`. Once you approve the plan, `/pick` moves
the item to in progress and continues with `/work`. When the item needs
refinement, `/pick` offers `/refine` or stops without changing it.
