---
title: /pick
description: Select one agent-shaped item, approve its plan, and start autonomous delivery.
---

`/pick` resolves one official item, shows its current context, and sends it to
planning. An approved plan starts autonomous delivery; an item containing
multiple delivery units can be handed to `/refine` without changing it first.

## What `/pick` does

<table class="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Pick</th>
      <td>Resolve and present one open official item whose labels include <code>agent-shaped</code>.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Plan</th>
      <td>Run <code>/plan</code> and receive either an approved plan or concise <code>needs-refinement</code> findings.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Route</th>
      <td>On refinement, ask the human to run <code>/refine</code> or stop; otherwise continue with the approved plan.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Start</th>
      <td>Move the item to <code>in progress</code>, report the observed status, and continue with <code>/work</code>.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
