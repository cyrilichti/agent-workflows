---
title: /pick
description: Pick your next work item, approve an implementation plan, and hand it off to /work.
---

`/pick` turns a ready item into active work. It lets you choose an item, reviews
its context, and sends it to `/plan`. Once you approve the plan, `/pick` moves
the item to in progress and hands its plan to `/work` in a separate task. The
new task identifies the handoff; `/pick` stops.
