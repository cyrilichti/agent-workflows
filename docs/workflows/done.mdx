---
title: /done
description: Squash-merge one exact request, then complete its official item.
---

`/done` is a separate, explicitly invoked completion workflow. It resolves the
item and request in standalone mode, or accepts a complete caller packet from
another authorized workflow.

An open request must be non-draft, mergeable at the unchanged head SHA, and
support squash merge. The item moves to its resolved completed state only after
the request is observed as merged.

Before preparing that completion, `/done` reads the official item again and
requires the exact `agent-inspected` label applied by a successful `/inspect`.
When the label is missing or cannot be verified, both entry modes stop without
confirmation or mutation and identify `/inspect` as the required next action.
