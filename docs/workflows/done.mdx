title: /done
description: Squash-merge one exact request, then complete its official item.
---

`/done` is a separate, explicitly invoked completion workflow. It resolves the
item and exact request, verifies that inspection and merge requirements still
hold, then asks once before changing anything.

## How `/done` completes delivery

`/done` stops on a changed or blocked request. It never modifies the delivered
work, deploys, releases, retries, or invokes another workflow.

## What `/done` does

<table className="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Preflight</th>
      <td>Read the current item and exact request, require <code>agent-inspected</code>, and verify the remaining completion operations.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Confirm</th>
      <td>Present the exact identities, states, merge method, and remaining mutations for one human decision.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Merge</th>
      <td>Recheck the unchanged request and squash-merge it when it is still open and mergeable.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Finish</th>
      <td>After observing the merged request, move the official item to <code>done</code> and report both results.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
