---
title: /ready
description: Verify completed work, confirm its push, and promote its request for human review.
---

`/ready` receives completed work from `/work` or lets you select a recent plan.
When the caller does not provide the request, it asks for the exact pull-request
number or merge-request IID instead of searching for one.

## How this workflow is structured

<table className="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Context</th>
      <td>Use caller context or select a completed plan and exact request.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Preflight</th>
      <td>Check terminal todos, clean worktree, branch, upstream, and request.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Validate</th>
      <td>Rerun the plan validation at the current HEAD.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Assess</th>
      <td>Have a read-only specialist compare the complete diff with the Objective and Expected Outcome.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>5</td>
      <th scope="row">Confirm</th>
      <td>Show the exact request body and mutations before asking once.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>6</td>
      <th scope="row">Promote</th>
      <td>Push, promote the request, attempt item review best-effort, report, and stop without invoking /review.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
