---
title: /inspect
description: Independently inspect one exact request snapshot and publish only the confirmed result.
---

`/inspect` is a standalone, manually invoked workflow. It resolves one official
item and its pull or merge request, inspects a frozen snapshot, and lets you
accept, reject, or modify every finding before anything is published.

No delivery workflow invokes `/inspect`. After an `approve` verdict is observed
as successfully published, `/inspect` offers to hand its compact completion
context directly to `/done`.

## How this workflow is structured

<table className="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Item</th>
      <td>Resolve an exact ID or title search; otherwise select among at most ten review items.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Request</th>
      <td>Resolve one open, non-draft request from its backlink or exact ID.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Snapshot</th>
      <td>Freeze the complete request state and head SHA.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Inspect</th>
      <td>Produce a complete SHA-bound result with persistent finding IDs.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>5</td>
      <th scope="row">Curate</th>
      <td>Accept, reject, or modify every finding in grouped decision passes.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>6</td>
      <th scope="row">Confirm</th>
      <td>Confirm the SHA, verdict, and accepted finding counts without repeating their bodies.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>7</td>
      <th scope="row">Publish</th>
      <td>Reject a stale SHA or publish and observe the confirmed grouped review.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>8</td>
      <th scope="row">Complete</th>
      <td>After a successfully published approval, choose whether to continue to <code>/done</code> with the preserved context.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Throughout the inspection cycle, `/inspect` never modifies code, items, commits,
branches, or existing comments. It never invokes `/work`, and never pushes,
merges, deploys, or releases itself. `/done` retains its separate preflight and
mutation confirmation after an eligible handoff.
