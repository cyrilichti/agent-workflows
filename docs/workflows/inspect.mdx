---
title: /inspect
description: Inspect and publish one exact request snapshot autonomously.
---

`/inspect` receives the delivery context from `/ready`, or in standalone mode
asks for the plan, item, and exact request. It binds one complete review to one
frozen request snapshot and publishes every valid finding without manual
curation.

## What `/inspect` does

<table className="workflow-steps">
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <th scope="row">Snapshot</th>
      <td>Freeze the complete open request, its review activity, authoritative plan, and head SHA.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <th scope="row">Inspect</th>
      <td>Use the reviewer profile to produce one complete SHA-bound result with persistent finding IDs.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <th scope="row">Publish</th>
      <td>Publish each unseen finding once for the unchanged SHA and observe the provider result.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>4</td>
      <th scope="row">Resolve</th>
      <td>Return blocking findings to <code>/work</code>; otherwise apply <code>agent-inspected</code> and stop without invoking <code>/done</code>.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
